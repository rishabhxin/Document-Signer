Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about creating signatures.

What is a signature generator? +

A signature generator is an online tool that lets you draw or type your personal digital signature and download it as a clean image file for use in PDFs, contracts, Word documents, and emails.

Is the Open Sign signature generator free to use? +

Yes. You can draw, type, customize, and download unlimited signatures directly in your browser with no watermarks and no sign-up required.

Can I draw my own signature on mobile or tablet? +

Yes. The drawing canvas supports mouse, laptop trackpads, touchscreens, and stylus pens smoothly across phones, tablets, and desktops.

What is the difference between PNG and JPEG downloads? +

PNG exports with a transparent background so your signature blends cleanly over contract lines and PDFs without a white box. JPEG exports with a crisp white background and smaller file size, ideal for email attachments and general sharing.

Is my signature saved or stored on any server? +

No. Everything runs 100% locally inside your browser. Your signature strokes, typed name, and exported images are never uploaded to or stored on any server.

Can I customize the style, thickness, and ink color? +

Yes. Choose from six ink colors, adjust fine/medium/bold stroke weight when drawing, or switch between six curated handwriting styles when typing your signature.

How do I use my generated signature on a PDF? +

Click "Use to Sign a PDF in Open Sign" to carry your signature straight into our PDF editor, or download the transparent PNG and insert it into any PDF or Word document.

How do I add my signature to Gmail or Outlook? +

Download your signature as a PNG or JPEG, open your email client's signature settings, and insert the image right above your name and title.

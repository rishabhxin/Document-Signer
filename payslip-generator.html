Payroll reference

Key components of an Indian salary slip.

Understand the standard earnings and statutory deductions that make up monthly employee compensation.

Earnings

  • Basic Salary The core fixed component of salary and the foundation for calculating statutory contributions such as 12% EPF.
  • House Rent Allowance (HRA) Typically 40%–50% of Basic pay depending on metro/non-metro city; eligible for partial income tax exemption when filing ITR.
  • Dearness Allowance (DA) Percentage of Basic pay provided to offset inflation (most common in public-sector salaries) and fully taxable.
  • Performance & Special Allowance Variable or fixed incentive components paid to reward performance or balance CTC structure.
  • Other Allowances Additional employer-specific heads such as conveyance, medical, internet, or meal allowances.

Deductions

  • Employee Provident Fund (EPF) Statutory retirement contribution typically calculated at 12% of Basic salary and deposited into the employee's EPF/UAN account.
  • Professional Tax (PT) State-level tax levied on salaried individuals in applicable Indian states (such as Maharashtra, Karnataka, West Bengal, and Tamil Nadu).
  • Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) Income tax deducted monthly by the employer on behalf of the Income Tax Department based on the employee's tax regime and slab.
  • Why Payslips Matter Essential for ITR planning, formal proof of employment and visas, home/personal loan & credit card approvals, and future salary negotiations.
Frequently asked questions

Common questions about Open Sign Payslip Generator.

What is a payslip and who receives it? +

A payslip (or salary slip) is an official monthly document issued by an employer to salaried employees detailing their gross earnings, statutory deductions, attendance days, and net take-home pay.

Are payslips generated here Indian statutory-compliant? +

Yes. The layout includes organization details, employee info (ID, PAN, UAN, Bank A/C), Pay Period, Paid & LOP days, Payment Date, itemized Earnings & Deductions, Gross Pay, Net Pay, and Amount in Words.

Can I customize, add, or remove salary components? +

Yes. You can rename any earning or deduction head, delete unused components so they never appear on the payslip, or add unlimited custom earnings, deductions, and employee summary fields.

Is employee salary data stored on any server? +

No. Everything runs 100% locally inside your browser. Employee details, PAN/UAN numbers, salary amounts, and generated PDFs never leave your device unless you save a local draft or download a backup yourself.

How does Auto-EPF (12% of Basic) work? +

When enabled, Open Sign automatically computes 12% of your Basic Salary row and keeps the EPF deduction row synchronized whenever you update Basic pay.

Can I add an HR signature or company stamp to the payslip? +

Yes. Draw or upload an authorized signature/company stamp directly in Step 4, or click "Sign in Open Sign" to open the generated payslip PDF inside our full PDF signer.

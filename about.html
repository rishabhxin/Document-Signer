About Open Sign

Trusted digital agreement infrastructure for modern business

Open Sign is a modern document technology company dedicated to making agreement execution simple, verifiable, and private by architecture. We empower organizations and professionals to prepare and sign critical documents directly in their browser without compromising data confidentiality.

100% Client-side processing with zero external server storage
Zero Friction Instant browser-native workflows with no mandatory onboarding walls
Enterprise Ready Built for legal, HR, finance, and operations teams worldwide
Who we are

Reimagining how organizations handle sensitive documents.

Open Sign was founded on a clear premise: business agreements should move at the speed of modern teams without forcing organizations to surrender control of their confidential files to third-party cloud vaults.

Legacy document platforms add layers of complex administration, mandatory seat licenses, and opaque server-side storage for everyday signing tasks. We engineered a browser-first platform that delivers speed, clarity, and mathematical layout precision directly on the user's device.

Headquartered in Mumbai and serving founders, legal counsel, HR departments, and enterprises across India and globally, Open Sign combines disciplined product design with strict privacy-by-design engineering.

Our Guiding Principle
“Your agreements belong to you. Software should remove friction from signing and closing business, never create a custody risk for your most sensitive records.”
Rishabh Pandey Entrepreneur · Open Sign
Core values

The principles behind every product decision.

Every capability we build is measured against four non-negotiable standards of trust, performance, and usability.

Privacy by Architecture

Document rendering, analysis, and final file generation occur locally within your browser session. Confidential contracts and financial records are never uploaded to or retained on remote servers.

Operational Velocity

We eliminate multi-step setup walls and bloated dashboards so teams can prepare, review, and execute time-sensitive agreements in seconds rather than days.

Precision & Reliability

From exact page-dimension preservation to multi-device rendering fidelity, our platform ensures every exported document looks crisp and authoritative across desktop, tablet, and mobile.

Universal Accessibility

Designed for organizations of every scale, from independent consultants to distributed enterprises, working seamlessly in any modern browser with zero software installation.

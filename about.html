Why we built Open Sign

Agreement software without friction or compromise.

Traditional signing suites slow teams down with bloated interfaces and unnecessary server storage. Open Sign delivers enterprise-grade signing precision with a streamlined experience anyone can use immediately.

Intelligent Smart Sign engine

Our Smart Sign technology analyses uploaded contracts, including multi-party agreements and scanned documents, to identify signature, name, date, and initials fields with confidence scoring before you sign.

Security & confidentiality first

Sensitive legal agreements, HR offers, and client contracts stay directly on your device. Rendering, Smart Sign analysis, OCR, and PDF export run locally inside your browser with zero server storage.

Pixel-accurate document fidelity

Whether you sign portrait contracts, landscape forms, or rotated scans, Open Sign preserves original page dimensions, orientation, and typography with exact normalized coordinate mapping.

Designed for everyday velocity

Built for founders, legal counsel, HR teams, agencies, and freelancers alike, so closing deals, onboarding hires, and approving agreements happens wherever work takes place.

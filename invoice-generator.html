Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Open Sign Invoice Generator.

Is Open Sign Invoice Generator GST-compliant for India? +

Yes. It supports seller and client GSTIN fields, HSN/SAC codes, Indian State Place of Supply codes, and automatic intra-state (CGST + SGST) vs. inter-state (IGST) tax calculations, plus amount in words in Lakhs and Crores.

Can I use this if I am not GST-registered or billing overseas clients? +

Yes. Switch the Tax System dropdown to "Standard Tax / VAT" or "No Tax / Non-GST Registered" and choose from INR, USD, EUR, GBP, AED, SGD, AUD, or CAD at any time.

How does the UPI payment QR code work? +

Enter your UPI ID (such as business@okaxis) and the tool generates a standard UPI payment QR code locally in your browser pre-filled with the balance due. Clients can scan it with GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM.

Is my invoice or financial data uploaded to any server? +

Never. All calculations, QR code rendering, PDF generation, and optional draft saves stay 100% locally inside your browser on your own device.

Can I add my digital signature to the invoice? +

Yes. Draw or upload your authorized signature right in Section 6, or generate one in Open Sign's Signature Generator, it carries over automatically. You can also open the exported invoice directly in Open Sign's full PDF signer.

Can I create quotes, proforma invoices, or delivery challans? +

Yes. Change the Document Type in Step 1 to generate a Tax Invoice, Commercial Invoice, Quote / Estimate, Proforma Invoice, Delivery Challan, or Payment Receipt, and export a backup JSON or Tally-friendly XML anytime.

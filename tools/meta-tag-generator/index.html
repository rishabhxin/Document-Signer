    Frequently asked questions

    Frequently Asked Questions

    1. What are meta tags? +

    Meta tags are HTML elements placed inside the <head> section of a webpage that communicate structured metadata, such as page descriptions, indexing rules, viewport settings, and social sharing cards, to search engines and social networks.

    2. Why are meta tags important for SEO? +

    Well-structured title tags, meta descriptions, canonical URLs, and robots directives help search engines understand how to index your pages, avoid duplicate URL confusion, and display clear, relevant snippets that encourage users to click.

    3. How do I generate meta tags for my website? +

    Enter your page title, description, URL, and optional advanced or social settings in the form above. Open Sign generates escaped, valid HTML code live as you type so you can copy or download it immediately.

    4. What is the ideal meta title length? +

    A common editing guideline is 50–70 characters so your core message fits comfortably on most desktop and mobile screens. However, Google measures pixel width rather than a strict character cap and may shorten or rewrite titles depending on the search query.

    5. What is the recommended meta description length? +

    Aim for roughly 120–160 characters as a practical guideline. Keep your summary concise and accurate, while keeping in mind that search engines often generate dynamic snippets from page text based on what a user searched for.

    6. What is a canonical URL? +

    A canonical tag (<link rel="canonical" href="https://www.example.com/page">) tells search engines which URL is the primary, preferred version of a page when similar or duplicate URLs exist (such as URLs with tracking parameters).

    7. What is the difference between robots.txt and robots meta tags? +

    A robots.txt file controls which URLs search engine bots are allowed to crawl, whereas a robots meta tag (<meta name="robots" content="index, follow">) gives page-level indexing and snippet instructions (like index, noindex, follow, or nosnippet) once a page is crawled.

    8. What are Open Graph tags? +

    Open Graph meta tags (og:title, og:description, og:image, og:url, og:type) define how your webpage appears when shared on platforms like Facebook, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, and Slack.

    9. What are Twitter/X Card tags? +

    Twitter/X Card tags (twitter:card, twitter:title, twitter:description, twitter:image) control whether shared links appear as a compact summary card or a large featured-image card on X.

    10. Where should I add generated meta tags? +

    Paste the generated meta tags inside the <head>...</head> section of your webpage's HTML file, or add them through your CMS, framework head component, or SEO settings panel.

    11. Are meta keywords still important for Google SEO? +

    No. Google Search has confirmed for years that it does not use the <meta name="keywords"> tag in web ranking, which is why Open Sign omits deprecated keyword tags by default to keep your HTML clean.

    12. Is Open Sign Meta Tag Generator free? +

    Yes. Open Sign Meta Tag Generator is 100% free to use right in your browser with no account required, and your inputs are never uploaded or stored on any server.

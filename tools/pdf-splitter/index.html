    Whether you need a single signature page or separate files for every chapter, choose the exact splitting workflow that fits your document.

    Split into Equal Parts

    Frequently asked questions

    Frequently Asked Questions

    1. How can I split a PDF file online for free? +

    Drop your PDF into the upload box above or click Browse PDF, choose your preferred splitting mode (Extract Selected Pages, Split Every Page, Split by Page Ranges, or Split into Equal Parts), click Split PDF, and download your generated files.

    2. Can I extract specific pages from a PDF? +

    Yes. Use Extract Selected Pages and either click the page checkboxes on the thumbnail grid or enter page numbers and ranges like 1, 3, 5-8 to create a new PDF with only those pages.

    3. Can I split every page into a separate PDF? +

    Yes. Select the Split Every Page mode to separate an N-page PDF into N individual single-page PDF documents (page-1.pdf, page-2.pdf, etc.).

    4. How do I split a PDF using page ranges? +

    Choose Split by Page Ranges and enter comma-separated ranges such as 1-5, 7-10, 12. Each comma-separated entry creates its own separate PDF file.

    5. Can I divide a PDF into equal parts? +

    Yes. Select Split into Equal Parts and specify how many output PDFs you want. If the total page count does not divide evenly, Open Sign distributes the remainder pages across the first parts and displays the exact breakdown.

    6. Is Open Sign PDF Splitter free? +

    Yes. Open Sign PDF Splitter is completely free to use directly in your browser with no sign-up, subscription, or watermarks.

    7. Are my PDF files uploaded to a server? +

    No. All page previews, range parsing, and PDF splitting execute 100% locally in your browser. Open Sign never uploads or stores your PDF files on any server.

    8. Will splitting a PDF affect its quality? +

    No. Pages are copied directly at the PDF object level without rasterizing them into images, so original text sharpness, vector graphics, page dimensions, and portrait or landscape orientations remain intact.

    9. Can I download multiple split PDFs in a ZIP file? +

    Yes. Whenever two or more PDFs are generated, you can download each PDF individually or click Download All as ZIP to save all split documents in one archive.

    10. Can I split a password-protected PDF? +

    Password-protected or encrypted PDFs cannot be split while locked. Unlock or remove the password protection first, then upload the unlocked file to split it.

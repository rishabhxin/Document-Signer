    KPI calculation formulas

    How to Calculate KPI Scores

    A Key Performance Indicator (KPI) measures how closely an actual result meets a defined target. Depending on whether higher or lower values are favorable, use one of three formulas:

    Higher is better: (Actual ÷ Target) × 100
    Lower is better: (Target ÷ Actual) × 100
    Binary KPI: Completed = 100%, Not Completed = 0%

    Multiply the resulting achievement percentage (subject to your achievement cap, such as 120%) by the KPI's weightage divided by 100 to obtain the Weighted KPI Score.

    Weighted KRA aggregation

    How to Calculate KRA Scores

    Each Key Result Area (KRA) groups related KPIs whose internal weightages total 100%. Summing the weighted KPI scores gives the KRA score. Then multiply each KRA score by its KRA weightage (where all KRAs total 100%) to find the overall employee performance score:

    KRA Score = Σ (Weighted KPI Scores)
    Overall Score = Σ (KRA Score × KRA Weightage ÷ 100)

    If any KPI is missing a target or actual value, Open Sign marks that KRA as Incomplete rather than silently treating missing numbers as zero.

    Core HR concepts

    KPI vs KRA Explained

    KRA (Key Result Area) defines what major outcomes a role is accountable for, such as Sales Performance, Customer Satisfaction, or Project Delivery. KPI (Key Performance Indicator) defines how success within that KRA is measured numerically, such as ₹5,00,000 Monthly Revenue or 95% On-Time Delivery. Together, KRAs provide strategic focus while KPIs provide objective measurement.

    Frequently asked questions

    Frequently Asked Questions

    1. What is the difference between KRA and KPI? +

    A KRA (Key Result Area) is a broad responsibility area assigned to an employee (such as Sales Performance or Quality of Work), while a KPI (Key Performance Indicator) is a quantifiable target inside that KRA used to measure actual achievement.

    2. Why must KRA and KPI weightages total 100%? +

    Weightages ensure every objective contributes proportionally to the final score. If KRA or KPI weightages do not add up to 100%, the weighted average would under- or over-state employee performance. Use the Auto-Split buttons to balance weights quickly.

    3. How do lower-is-better KPIs work? +

    For metrics like support response time, bug count, or cost per lead where smaller numbers are better, select "Lower is better". The calculator divides Target by Actual (Target ÷ Actual × 100) and safely handles zero values.

    4. Why is there a 120% achievement cap by default? +

    Capping individual KPI achievement at 120% prevents a single extreme outlier from masking underperformance in other critical KPIs. You can adjust the cap percentage or turn off capping completely under Achievement Cap & Rating Thresholds.

    5. Can I customize the performance rating thresholds? +

    Yes. Expand Section 2 (Achievement Cap & Rating Thresholds) to edit the minimum percentage required for Outstanding, Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations, and Needs Improvement.

    6. Can I download the KPI & KRA report as PDF or Excel? +

    Yes. Click Download PDF Report for a formatted summary with the Open Sign logo and calculation methodology, Export Excel (.xlsx) for a 2-sheet workbook, or Export CSV for raw spreadsheet analysis.

    7. Is this KPI & KRA Calculator free and private? +

    Yes. It is 100% free with no login required, and all calculations and file exports happen locally inside your web browser without sending employee data to any server.

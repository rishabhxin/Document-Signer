    Frequently asked questions

    Frequently Asked Questions

    1. How do I generate a QR code online for free? +

    Select a QR code type above, type in your URL, Wi-Fi details, contact card, or text, customize colors and size if desired, and click Download PNG, Download SVG, or Download JPG. No sign-up is required.

    2. Can I create a QR code for a website? +

    Yes. Choose Website URL, paste your HTTPS or HTTP link, optionally add a campaign name or query parameters, and download your QR code immediately.

    3. Can I create a QR code for Wi-Fi? +

    Yes. Select the Wi-Fi option, enter your network name (SSID), password, and security type (WPA/WPA2, WEP, or None). Your Wi-Fi credentials stay strictly inside your browser and are never sent to any server.

    4. Can I add my logo to a QR code? +

    Yes. Upload a PNG, JPG, or WebP logo under Customize Design, Size & Logo. Open Sign centers your logo within safe limits and automatically increases error correction to High (H), though you should always test with multiple scanners before printing.

    5. Can I customize QR code colors? +

    Yes. Use the built-in color presets or pick custom foreground and background HEX colors. For reliable scanning, always keep a strong contrast between dark foreground modules and a light background.

    6. Can I download QR codes in SVG format? +

    Yes. Click Download SVG to export a clean, scalable vector graphic (opensign-qr-code.svg) that stays sharp at any print size without pixelation.

    7. Do Open Sign QR codes expire? +

    No. The QR codes generated by this tool are static QR codes, meaning your content is encoded directly into the QR pattern itself and does not expire on its own. However, if you encode a destination URL or resource that later changes, moves, or becomes unavailable, that link will stop working.

    8. Can I create WhatsApp QR codes? +

    Yes. Select WhatsApp, enter your phone number with country code (for example 911234567890), and add an optional pre-filled greeting message to generate a standard https://wa.me/ click-to-chat QR code.

    9. Can I create QR codes for contact details? +

    Yes. Choose Contact / vCard to encode your name, organization, job title, phone number, email, website, and address in standard vCard 3.0 format.

    10. Are my QR code details uploaded to a server? +

    No. All QR matrix generation, logo rendering, scan verification, and file exports run 100% locally in your browser. Open Sign never uploads or stores your QR content or logo.

    11. What is QR code error correction? +

    Error correction adds redundant data modules so a QR code remains readable even if part of it is obscured or worn: Low (L, ~7%), Medium (M, ~15%), Quartile (Q, ~25%), and High (H, ~30%). Higher levels increase module density.

    12. How do I test whether my QR code works? +

    Click Verify Scan Locally in the validation panel to run an immediate browser-based QR decode check on your generated image, and also scan the preview or printed proof with iOS and Android camera apps before mass printing.

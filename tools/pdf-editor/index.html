Complete document studio

PDF Editing Features

Open Sign PDF Editor combines text editing, visual whiteout replacement, optional browser OCR for scanned pages, image and logo placement, freehand drawing, shapes, sticky notes, AcroForm filling, page management, watermarks, headers, footers, and metadata editing in a single workspace.

Text & visual branding

How to Add Text and Images to PDFs

Select Add Text in the ribbon and click anywhere on the page to type inline, then customize font family, size, bold/italic/underline, color, alignment, and background in the Properties panel. Use Add Image to insert PNG, JPG, or WebP logos, stamps, or product photos with aspect-ratio lock and crop controls.

Visual signing

How to Sign a PDF

Open the Insert & Forms tab and click Signature to draw with a mouse or touch screen, type a signature appearance, or upload a signature scan. Visual signature images are clearly distinguished from certificate-based cryptographic digital signatures.

Page organization

How to Organize PDF Pages

Use the left thumbnail sidebar and Organize Pages tab to drag-and-drop reorder pages, rotate clockwise or counterclockwise, add blank pages, duplicate or delete pages, crop margins, merge another PDF, extract selected pages, or split every page into a ZIP archive.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is my PDF uploaded to a server when I edit it? +

No. Rendering, editing, OCR, form filling, and PDF rebuilding all run 100% locally inside your browser memory. Open Sign never uploads or stores your documents.

2. How does existing PDF text editing work? +

When you choose Edit Existing Text, Open Sign detects extractable text spans on the page so you can click any span to place a cleanly matched cover-and-replace text box without corrupting complex embedded PDF fonts. For scanned PDFs, use optional browser OCR (Tesseract.js) and review recognized text carefully.

3. Does the editor preserve original PDF quality instead of rasterizing? +

Yes. Export uses pdf-lib to copy your original vector and text PDF pages directly and draw your edits on top, preserving crisp text and original page dimensions.

4. Are signatures created here cryptographic digital signatures? +

No. Signatures placed in the PDF Editor are visual electronic signature images, not certificate-based cryptographic digital signatures (such as DSC / PKI tokens).

5. Can I fill existing PDF forms or add new form fields? +

Yes. Click Fill Forms under the Insert & Forms tab to fill detected text fields, checkboxes, radio buttons, and dropdowns, or insert new interactive AcroForm fields onto any page.

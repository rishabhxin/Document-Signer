    Frequently asked questions

    Frequently Asked Questions

    1. How can I merge multiple PDF files into one? +

    Click Upload PDF Files or drag and drop two or more PDFs into the tool above. Arrange them in your desired sequence by dragging rows or clicking Move Up / Move Down, then click Merge PDF and download the combined file.

    2. Is Open Sign PDF Merger free? +

    Yes. Open Sign PDF Merger is free to use in your browser with no account, no subscription, and no watermarks added to your merged PDF.

    3. Can I rearrange PDF pages before merging? +

    You can rearrange the exact order of your uploaded PDF files before merging using drag-and-drop or the Move Up and Move Down buttons. Pages within each individual PDF stay in their original sequence.

    4. Is there a limit on the number of PDF files? +

    Open Sign does not impose an artificial file-count cap, and you can merge two, five, or dozens of PDFs. Practical limits depend on your browser and device memory since all processing happens locally.

    5. Are my PDF files uploaded to a server? +

    No. All file validation and PDF merging run 100% locally inside your web browser. Open Sign never uploads, transmits, or stores your PDF files on any server.

    6. Can I merge large PDF files? +

    Yes, large PDFs are supported up to the memory capacity of your browser and device. Very large files (for example over 50–100 MB combined) may take a few extra seconds to process locally, especially on mobile devices.

    7. Does the merged PDF maintain the original page quality? +

    Yes. Pages are copied directly at the PDF object level rather than rasterized into images, preserving original text sharpness, vector graphics, page sizes, and portrait or landscape orientations.

    8. Can I merge password-protected PDF files? +

    Password-protected or encrypted PDFs cannot be merged directly while locked. Unlock or remove the password restriction from the file first, then upload it to merge smoothly.

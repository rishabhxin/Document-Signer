Frequently asked questions

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I convert a PDF to JPG online? +

Drop your PDF into the upload box above or click Browse PDF, choose which pages to convert, set your preferred image quality and DPI resolution, click Convert to JPG, and download individual JPG files or a ZIP archive.

2. Can I convert only selected PDF pages? +

Yes. Choose Convert Selected Pages to click individual page checkboxes on the thumbnail grid, or choose Convert Page Ranges to enter specific pages and ranges like 1-5, 7, 9-12.

3. Can I convert an entire PDF into JPG images? +

Yes. Keep Convert All Pages selected (the default) and click Convert to JPG to render every page of your document as a separate JPG image.

4. What image resolutions are available? +

You can choose 72 DPI, 150 DPI (default), 200 DPI, 300 DPI, or enter a custom DPI value between 36 and 400 DPI. Higher DPI settings produce sharper images and larger file sizes.

5. Can I download all converted images together? +

Yes. When two or more pages are converted, click Download All as ZIP (or select specific images in the gallery and click Download Selected as ZIP) to package them into one ZIP archive (default filename: opensign-pdf-to-jpg.zip).

6. Is Open Sign PDF to JPG Converter free? +

Yes. Open Sign PDF to JPG Converter is 100% free to use in your browser with no account registration and no watermarks.

7. Are my PDF files uploaded to a server? +

No. All PDF parsing, page rendering, and JPG encoding occur locally on your device inside your web browser. Open Sign never uploads or stores your documents on any server.

8. Will converting PDF to JPG reduce quality? +

JPG uses lossy image compression, so very low quality or 72 DPI settings can soften small text. Choosing High Quality (95%) at 200 or 300 DPI preserves crisp visual detail.

9. Can I convert scanned PDF documents? +

Yes. Both native digital PDFs and scanned image-based PDFs are rendered accurately onto a white background and exported as standard JPG images.

10. Will the converted JPG images contain selectable text? +

No. Converting a PDF to JPG creates raster pixel images, so text inside the output JPG files cannot be highlighted or copied as text.

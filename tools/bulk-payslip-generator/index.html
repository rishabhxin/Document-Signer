Transparent arithmetic

Salary Calculation Explanation

Every payslip uses decimal-safe arithmetic directly on your uploaded figures without silently applying outdated or assumed statutory PF, ESI, or TDS rates:

  • Total Earnings: Sum of Basic Salary, all mapped allowances, bonus, incentives, overtime, and custom earnings.
  • Total Deductions: Sum of uploaded PF, ESI, Professional Tax, TDS, loan, advance, and custom deductions.
  • Net Salary Payable: Total Earnings minus Total Deductions, displayed in Indian currency words (Lakhs & Crores).

Frequently asked questions

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is my employees' salary data uploaded to Open Sign servers? +

No. Spreadsheet parsing, salary calculations, PDF generation, and ZIP packaging run 100% locally inside your web browser. Data is never uploaded to any server or saved to browser storage.

2. Can I use my own Excel or CSV column headers? +

Yes. Step 4 automatically detects clear column matches, flags ambiguous headers so nothing is silently guessed, and lets you manually map standard fields or assign extra columns as Custom Earnings or Custom Deductions.

3. Does the Bulk Payslip Generator automatically deduct PF, ESI, or TDS? +

No. To prevent compliance errors from outdated or assumed statutory slabs, the tool uses the exact deduction amounts present in your uploaded spreadsheet.

4. How many employees can I generate payslips for at once? +

The generator processes PDFs in non-blocking browser batches so batches of 100+ employees run smoothly without freezing the interface, and failed items can be retried individually.

5. What download formats are supported? +

You can download each employee's individual PDF, download all PDFs packaged in a single ZIP folder, download one combined multi-page PDF (one employee per page), and export a CSV salary summary.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I create an employee attendance sheet? +

Select a template above, add your organization name and employee list, pick the month and year, mark daily statuses in the attendance calendar, and click Export Excel (.xlsx), Export PDF, or Export CSV.

2. Can I create monthly attendance sheets for multiple employees? +

Yes. You can add unlimited employees manually or import them in bulk via CSV, filter by department, and click any employee's name to open their individual monthly attendance report.

3. Can I export attendance sheets to Excel? +

Yes. Click Export Excel (.xlsx) to generate a multi-sheet workbook containing Monthly Attendance, Employee Summary, and Holiday List tabs with frozen header rows and formatted totals.

4. Can I download attendance reports as PDF? +

Yes. Click Export PDF for a landscape multi-page register with your logo, repeating table headers, status legend, page numbers, and HR signature line—or export an individual PDF from any employee's detail modal.

5. How is attendance percentage calculated? +

Attendance % = (Counted Present Days ÷ Eligible Working Days) × 100. Eligible Working Days exclude scheduled weekly offs and holidays. Present, WFH, and On Duty count as 1 day, Half Day counts as 0.5 day, and you can toggle whether Approved Leave counts toward the percentage.

6. Can I mark half-day attendance? +

Yes. Click any day cell and choose HD (Half Day). It automatically counts as 0.5 working day in monthly totals and attendance percentage calculations.

7. Can I add holidays and weekly offs? +

Yes. Configure your weekly off days (such as Sunday-only or Saturday & Sunday), add custom holidays in the Holiday Calendar section, and click Apply Weekly Offs or Apply Holidays to populate the grid without counting them as absences.

8. Can I import employees using CSV? +

Yes. Download the Sample Employee CSV in Section 2 to see the exact columns, fill in your roster, and click Import Employees CSV. You can also import historical attendance records via CSV with a live preview before applying.

9. Is the attendance sheet generator free? +

Yes. Open Sign Attendance Sheet Generator is 100% free to use with no sign-up, subscription, or watermarks on exported files.

10. Is employee attendance data uploaded to a server? +

No. All calculations, CSV parsing, Excel/PDF exports, and auto-saved records stay strictly inside your browser's local IndexedDB storage. Open Sign never uploads or stores your data on any server.

11. Can I create attendance sheets for schools? +

Yes. Select the School / Student template at the top of the dashboard to switch to student roll numbers, classes/sections, and teacher/principal sign-off labels.

12. Can I save and continue editing attendance records? +

Yes. Changes are automatically saved in your browser's local IndexedDB storage so you can return later and continue editing, or download a JSON backup file anytime to transfer across devices.

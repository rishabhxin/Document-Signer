Frequently asked questions

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How can I compress a PDF file online for free? +

Drop your PDF into the upload box above or click Browse PDF, choose Low (Best Quality), Medium (Recommended), or High (Smallest File), click Compress PDF, and download your optimized file once the before-and-after comparison appears.

2. Will compressing a PDF reduce its quality? +

Structural optimization is lossless and keeps visual quality unchanged. Lossy image recompression (used especially in Medium and High modes) reduces image resolution and JPEG quality to cut file size, so stronger compression can make fine text or photos look softer.

3. Can I compress a PDF without uploading it to a server? +

Yes. Open Sign runs PDF analysis, canvas image optimization, and PDF rebuilding 100% inside your browser. Your document never leaves your device.

4. What is the difference between low, medium and high compression? +

Low (Best Quality) targets up to 90% JPEG quality at 200 DPI and prefers lossless structural optimization first. Medium (Recommended) balances size and clarity at 75% quality and 150 DPI. High (Smallest File) targets 55% quality at 100 DPI for maximum size reduction on image-heavy files.

5. Can I compress scanned PDF documents? +

Yes. Scanned PDFs usually store large uncompressed or high-DPI page images, so Medium or High compression often achieves significant size reductions on scanned contracts, IDs, and forms.

6. Does Open Sign support large PDF files? +

Open Sign supports PDF files up to 100 MB directly in the browser. Because compression runs locally on your device, very large multi-page PDFs may take longer on phones or low-memory laptops.

7. Can I compress a PDF without losing text quality? +

Yes. Check the "Preserve selectable text & structure only" option before clicking Compress PDF. This runs lossless structural optimization without rasterizing pages, keeping text sharp and selectable (though file-size savings on image-heavy PDFs will be smaller).

8. Why did my compressed PDF become larger? +

PDFs that are already tightly optimized or contain mostly compact vector text (such as clean digital invoices) can sometimes grow when rasterized into images. Whenever our output is not smaller than your original file, Open Sign notifies you transparently and lets you retain your original PDF.

9. Will compression affect digital signatures? +

Yes. Any modification or recompression changes the underlying PDF bytes, which invalidates existing cryptographic digital signatures. Always compress your PDF first before digitally signing it.

10. Can I compress password-protected PDFs? +

No. Encrypted or password-protected PDFs cannot be processed while locked. Remove the password protection first, then upload the unlocked PDF to compress it.

About

Open Sign is a free, open-source way to sign a PDF. Everything happens in your browser, so your document stays yours.

  1. 01
    Open

    Choose a PDF. It's read on your device and never uploaded.

  2. 02
    Sign

    Draw, type or upload a signature, then click where it belongs. Add text or a date too.

  3. 03
    Download

    Save the signed PDF. Nothing to install, no account to create.
